# PHASE 9 — Research Contribution

> *What here is genuinely new, what is publishable, what competitors miss, and what IP could emerge.*

## 9.1 What is genuinely novel in ARGUS

The individual evidence modules are mostly known forensics. **The novelty is in the system-level reframing and three specific mechanisms:**

1. **Reliability as a first-class, calibrated, per-module quantity decoupled from confidence.** The literature conflates "how sure is the model" with "how readable is the image." ARGUS's formal separation — with **empirically calibrated degradation transfer functions** that map an image's quality vector to each module's *maximum achievable reliability* — is, to our knowledge, not how production detectors are built. The self-silencing behavior (a module that *knows* it cannot work) is the novel, demonstrable contribution.
2. **Reliability-scaled Bayesian belief combination with an open-set OOD gate** — fusing forensic evidence such that an uninformative or out-of-distribution channel contributes *exactly zero* log-odds, and the system *abstains* rather than hallucinating confidence on unseen generators. This is a principled answer to the arms-race trap that the classifier paradigm cannot give.
3. **Tri-axial output (Authenticity / Trust / Risk) with an explicit contradiction graph** as the explanation substrate — separating "is it real," "can we tell," and "should you act," and treating contradiction as decision-relevant signal rather than noise to average away.

## 9.2 Publishable contributions

- **Paper 1 (systems/forensics venue — e.g., WIFS, IH&MMSec, CVPR Workshops on Media Forensics):** *"Reliability-Aware Evidence Fusion for Open-Set Image Authenticity."* Core claim: calibration-under-shift and selective-risk dominate raw accuracy as the right objective; demonstrate graceful degradation + abstention beating single classifiers on LOGO + laundering. Strong, defensible, reproducible (open data).
- **Paper 2 (methods):** *"Degradation Transfer Functions: Calibrating Forensic Module Reliability to Image Quality."* The self-supervised reliability-calibration method — generalizable beyond authenticity (applies to any quality-dependent forensic signal).
- **Paper 3 (evaluation/position):** *"Beyond Accuracy: Calibration and Abstention as the Correct Objectives for Deepfake Detection in the Open Set."* A benchmark + metric proposal (LOGO + ECE-under-laundering + selective risk). High citation potential because it reframes how the field should measure progress.

## 9.3 What competitors are likely missing

- **They optimize accuracy on seen generators; ARGUS optimizes calibrated honesty on unseen ones.** The entire incumbent objective function is misaligned with the production reality.
- **They have no reliability layer** — they read dead channels confidently. This is the most exploitable gap.
- **They average contradictions into mush** instead of surfacing them as the most decision-relevant output.
- **They cannot abstain.** A system that must always answer will always be beatable; a system that can say "insufficient evidence" cannot be lured into a confident error.
- **They treat provenance/retrieval as side-features** rather than as the open-set backbone.
- **Their explanations are saliency maps over a black box**, not chains of independently verifiable forensic facts.

## 9.4 Intellectual property

- **Defensible IP:** (a) the degradation-transfer-function calibration pipeline and the reliability/confidence decoupling; (b) the reliability-scaled Bayesian+correction-head fusion with OOD gating and conformal abstention; (c) the contradiction-graph → tri-axial-scoring → grounded-explanation pipeline; (d) the curated, continuously-updated **known-fake / AI-gallery retrieval index** as a data moat (grows with use — a network effect competitors cannot quickly replicate).
- **Hardest-to-copy moat:** *not* any single algorithm (most are open) but the **calibration data + retrieval corpus + the operational discipline of honest abstention**. The corpus compounds; the calibration is a process, not a model checkpoint; and "we tell you when we don't know" is a *trust* moat that is hard for accuracy-chasers to adopt without rebuilding their whole stack.

# PHASE 10 — Critical Review (Hostile Peer Review + Redesign)

> *I now attack ARGUS as a hostile Reviewer 2 trying to reject the paper and kill the startup. Then I redesign to survive each attack.*

## 10.1 The attack

**Hidden assumptions**
- **A-1 (fatal-looking):** "ARGUS assumes physics/semantic consistency is durable. But generators are *already* improving at 3-D consistency (lighting-aware diffusion, geometry-conditioned models). Your 'durable backbone' is on the same decay curve as fingerprints — just slower. In 3 years your moat is gone too."
- **A-2:** "Retrieval is your strongest open-set signal, but it only works for images that have *already been seen online*. For a *novel* harmful image (the case that actually matters — a fresh fabricated 'evidence' photo) retrieval finds nothing and abstains. Your best module is useless exactly when stakes are highest."
- **A-3:** "Your reliability transfer functions are calibrated on *simulated* degradations. Real platform pipelines differ, are proprietary, and change constantly. Your reliability estimates are themselves miscalibrated, which poisons the whole fusion."
- **A-4:** "You demote the classifier and lean on physics/provenance, but on the *median* internet image — a laundered screenshot with no C2PA, no web match, low-res — *all* your durable modules are low-reliability and you abstain. An abstaining detector that abstains on the majority of real traffic is commercially worthless."

**Scientific weaknesses**
- **S-1:** Conditional-independence in the Bayesian core is false; the LightGBM correction head patches it but reintroduces the *closed-world* overfitting you criticized — the head is trained on seen generators and will mis-correct on unseen ones, *re-coupling* you to the arms race through the back door.
- **S-2:** Conformal abstention guarantees coverage *only under exchangeability* — which **fails under distribution shift**, exactly the open-set case. Your headline uncertainty guarantee is void precisely when invoked.
- **S-3:** "Implausible perfection" assumes real images are noisy — but modern phone computational-photography pipelines (denoise, HDR, deep-fusion) produce extremely clean, smoothed images. Your too-clean signal will **false-positive on every flagship smartphone photo.**

**Engineering risks**
- **E-1:** The VLM physics/semantic module is the most expensive, slowest, least reliable component and is doing the heaviest lifting on the open set. VLMs hallucinate; a hallucinated "six fingers" is a confident false positive with a plausible explanation — *worse* than a black box because it's persuasively wrong.
- **E-2:** Maintenance burden: 8+ modules, per-module calibration, a retrieval corpus, an OOD model each — operational complexity is an order of magnitude over a single classifier, on a "limited budget."

**Dataset biases**
- **D-1:** Open authentic sets (FFHQ, RAISE, Flickr-CC) skew Western, well-lit, high-end-camera, English-text. Reliability calibration and semantic checks will be biased; ARGUS will be *less reliable on under-represented populations and low-end devices* — an equity and liability problem.
- **D-2:** LOGO still only holds out *known* generators; truly novel *architectures* may break assumptions LOGO can't simulate. Your headline metric overstates open-set robustness.

**Scalability**
- **SC-1:** Retrieval index over "the indexed web" is infeasible at startup budget; a small index has poor coverage → abstention-heavy → see A-4.
- **SC-2:** Per-image cost (VLM + CLIP + FAISS + classical) is far higher than a single forward pass; at platform scale (billions/day) the economics break.

## 10.2 The redesign (point-by-point survival)

- **→ A-1 (durability decay):** *Concede the premise, fix the framing.* No evidence is eternally durable; ARGUS's claim is **relative half-life + graceful, honest decay**, not permanence. The architectural answer is that ARGUS is *built to absorb new evidence modules and re-weight via recalibration*, so as physics decays we add the next durable class (e.g., provenance adoption rising, hardware attestation, multi-image cross-checking). The reliability layer means a decaying module *down-weights itself* as its calibrated accuracy drops — the system **ages gracefully by construction** rather than silently failing. The moat is the *adaptive fusion framework*, not any single cue.
- **→ A-2 / A-4 (novel image, median traffic → abstention):** *This is the most important fix.* (1) Reframe abstention as the **product**, not a failure: for high-stakes users (newsrooms, courts, insurers) *"we cannot verify this — here is exactly what we could and couldn't check"* is **more valuable** than a coin-flip label, and is honestly differentiated. (2) Add **active evidence acquisition**: when durable modules are low-reliability, *request more* — original file instead of screenshot, other frames, source URL, a capture challenge — turning abstention into a guided workflow. (3) Maintain a **graded output even when abstaining** (Risk can be HIGH on contradiction alone, without a confident Authenticity). So ARGUS on a novel laundered image still says "high risk, low trust, here's why," not silence.
- **→ A-3 (sim-vs-real degradation):** Calibrate on **real** platform-laundered pairs collected cheaply at scale (upload/redownload through real platforms) and treat reliability functions as **continuously updated** from production feedback. Add **uncertainty *on the reliability estimate itself*** (a reliability with error bars), propagated into Trust — so miscalibrated reliability widens uncertainty rather than silently corrupting fusion.
- **→ S-1 (head re-couples to arms race):** Constrain the correction head to **monotonic, regularized** corrections and **exclude generator-specific features from it**; train it only to model *inter-module dependence among durable channels*, validated LOGO. Keep the Bayesian core (no learned generator coupling) as the **floor**: if the head disagrees strongly with the core on an OOD input, **trust the core and raise uncertainty**. The head can only *refine within* the durable evidence, never override it on novel inputs.
- **→ S-2 (conformal void under shift):** Replace/augment vanilla conformal with **Mondrian/group-conditional and weighted/shift-adaptive conformal** (importance-weighted to the test distribution), and — crucially — **do not claim a guarantee under shift**; claim *empirically validated* selective risk on LOGO + laundering. The honest framing (validated, not guaranteed) is itself the scientifically defensible position.
- **→ S-3 / D-1 (too-clean false positives on phones; demographic bias):** Make "implausible perfection" **device-and-pipeline-conditioned** — learn the noise/smoothing signature of *computational-photography pipelines* and treat *those* as a known authentic mode, not anomaly. Expand calibration/authentic data to **low-end devices, diverse populations, multilingual text, varied lighting**; report **per-subgroup reliability and error**, and *lower Trust* (don't guess) on under-represented inputs rather than confidently erring. Bias becomes a *measured, surfaced* limitation, not a hidden one.
- **→ E-1 (VLM hallucination):** Never let the VLM *assert* facts that drive the score. Use it as a **proposer**, then **verify geometrically** (a claimed shadow inconsistency must be confirmed by an actual light-direction/shadow-geometry estimator before it contributes LLR; a "six fingers" claim must pass a detector cross-check). VLM confidence is capped and its reliability is low unless corroborated by a non-VLM module. Grounded-only narration (Phase 7) already prevents hallucinated *explanations*.
- **→ E-2 / SC-1 / SC-2 (complexity & cost at scale):** **Tiered cascade.** Stage-0 = the cheap classical modules + metadata/C2PA + pHash (milliseconds, CPU) handle the easy majority. Only **ambiguous or high-stakes** images escalate to the expensive Stage-1 (CLIP/FAISS retrieval, VLM physics). This caps average cost near a single classifier's while reserving heavy forensics for the cases that need them. The retrieval index starts **domain-scoped** (the customer's relevant corpus + a curated known-fake/AI-gallery set), not "the whole web" — high precision where it matters, growing over time.
- **→ D-2 (LOGO overstates):** State it plainly as a limitation and add **truly novel-architecture holdouts** as they emerge; treat every new generator as a continuous external validation event. Never claim LOGO proves robustness to the *truly* unseen — claim it is the best available proxy and that the *architecture* (abstention + durable-evidence backbone) is what carries the residual risk.

## 10.3 Post-redesign architecture summary

The redesign changes ARGUS from "a clever fusion of forensics" into **"an adaptive, self-recalibrating, abstention-first evidence engine with a cheap-to-expensive cascade, device-conditioned anomaly models, geometrically-verified VLM proposals, and honestly-stated (not over-claimed) uncertainty."** Every hostile point is either conceded-and-reframed (durability, abstention, conformal guarantees) or engineered away (cascade cost, VLM hallucination, bias, sim-vs-real calibration).
